# Women_Ai
# 🌸 Women AI

## Your Personal AI Wellness, Lifestyle & Career Assistant

Women AI is an AI-powered personal assistant designed to provide personalized support for women's wellness, lifestyle, personal care, and career development.

The platform brings multiple useful features into one place, allowing users to create a personal profile, complete daily check-ins, interact with an AI assistant, receive lifestyle guidance, and get career and resume support.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Women AI is designed to make AI-based assistance more accessible and personalized for women.

Instead of using separate applications for different needs, Women AI combines:

- 💗 Wellness support
- 🧴 Skin and hair care guidance
- 🥗 Food and lifestyle guidance
- 🏃 Fitness support
- 👗 Fashion and lifestyle suggestions
- 💬 Personalized AI chat
- 📄 Resume assistance
- 💼 Career and interview support
- 📊 Daily check-ins and personalized information

The goal is to create a single AI-powered platform that can understand a user's profile and provide relevant assistance based on their needs.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Women often use different platforms for wellness, lifestyle, personal care, and career-related needs.

This can make information difficult to organize and personalize.

Women AI aims to provide a single AI-powered assistant where users can interact with one platform for multiple everyday needs.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Women AI combines user profile information, daily check-ins, and AI-powered conversations to provide personalized assistance.

The user can create a profile and interact with the AI according to their requirements.

The platform can provide guidance across areas such as:

**Profile → Daily Check-in → AI Chat → Personalized Guidance → Career Support**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 1. Personal Profile

Users can create a personal profile by entering relevant information.

This helps the assistant provide more personalized responses.

### 💗 2. Daily Women AI Check-in

Users can provide information about their daily state, including mood and other lifestyle-related information.

The check-in can help the assistant understand the user's current needs.

### 🤖 3. Personalized AI Chat

Users can ask questions and interact with the AI assistant.

The assistant can provide responses related to:

- Wellness
- Lifestyle
- Skin care
- Hair care
- Food
- Fitness
- Fashion
- Career
- Resume
- Interviews

### 🧴 4. Personal Care Guidance

Women AI provides AI-based general guidance related to:

- Skin care
- Hair care
- Body care
- Lifestyle habits

### 🥗 5. Food & Lifestyle Support

The assistant can provide general suggestions related to food, healthy lifestyle habits, and daily routines.

### 🏃 6. Fitness & Wellness

Users can ask the AI about general fitness, wellness, and lifestyle routines.

### 📄 7. Resume Assistant

Users can provide their resume information and receive AI-based assistance with:

- Resume improvement
- Skills
- Career profile
- Professional descriptions
- Interview preparation

### 💼 8. Career & Interview Support

Women AI can assist users with career-related questions and interview preparation.

### 📊 9. Personalized Dashboard

The platform is designed to organize user information and provide a personalized experience.
